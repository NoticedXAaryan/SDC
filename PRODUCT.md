# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Student club members, leads, and admins (specifically for Parul University's Student Developer Club). They use this to manage operations, register for events, check-in via QR, and track project submissions.

## Product Purpose
The Student Developer Club platform is our club's website and the place where members, leads, and admins coordinate everything — events, projects, certificates, and daily operations.

## Brand Personality
Space, cosmic, premium-observatory, restrained. Like "Comet Browser" by Perplexity. Not sci-fi gaudy. Confident and modern.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboard templates (no basic white/cream backgrounds)
- Loud, gaudy, neon sci-fi themes
- Overly cluttered or heavy card-based layouts

## Design Principles
- Dark-mode first with deep space backgrounds.
- Celestial gradient accents (purples, blues, warm nebula tones) to draw focus.
- Subtle orbital and gravitational motion that implies weight.
- Restraint over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for readability (WCAG AA).
- Reduced motion alternatives for orbital animations.
